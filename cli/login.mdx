---
title: "zend login"
description: "Pair the CLI with your Zend account by approving it on your phone."
---

```bash
zend login
```

This is the only way to authenticate. No flag or prompt accepts a typed API key.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The CLI starts a pairing session">
    It prints an approval link and a QR code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open it on your phone">
    The link opens in the Zend app, on an approval screen that names this CLI (`zend-cli on <hostname>`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve">
    Tap **Approve** and confirm it's you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The CLI saves its key">
    The CLI notices the approval, fetches its API key, and saves it to `~/.zend/config.json` with `0600` permissions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## When it doesn't finish

| Output | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Login was denied in the Zend App.` | You tapped deny. Nothing was saved |
| `Pairing session expired before it was approved.` | Sessions last 10 minutes. Run `zend login` again |

## Log out

Delete `~/.zend/config.json`, and revoke the key from another session with [`zend keys revoke`](/cli/keys#revoke).
