---
title: "zend logs tail"
description: "Show recent webhook deliveries for your account."
---

```bash
zend logs tail --limit 20
```

```text
2026-07-06T14:20:11Z  PaymentRequestSucceeded       delivered   200  attempts=1  https://yourapp.com/webhooks/zend
2026-07-06T14:15:03Z  PaymentRequestCreated         delivered   200  attempts=1  https://yourapp.com/webhooks/zend
2026-07-06T13:58:47Z  PaymentRequestFailed          failed      502  attempts=3  https://yourapp.com/webhooks/zend
```

Shows up to 50 of your most recent payment request webhook deliveries, newest first. `--limit` defaults to 50.

Use it to debug an endpoint that isn't receiving events before you add logging to your server.

<Note>
This shows payment request webhooks for your account. For a ZApp's webhooks, call [`GET /apps/server/events`](/reference/webhooks#catch-up) with the app key.
</Note>
