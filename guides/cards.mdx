---
title: "Cards"
description: "Show up in chats and the Activity feed with cards Zend draws for you."
---

Cards are how your app appears in chats and the Activity feed. You pick a template and fill its slots; Zend draws the card, adds your app's name, and shows the interaction's live state.

```ts
await zend.postCard({
  interactionId: game.id,
  template: "result",
  placement: "chat",
  idempotencyKey: `${game.id}:result`,
  slots: {
    title: "Tunde beat Danny at Accident",
    emoji: "😂",
    buttons: [{ label: "Challenge again", action: "start_interaction", type: "challenge" }],
  },
});
```

Needs the `post_card` permission, the template listed in `cardTypes`, and the user's consent to post.

## Templates

| Template | Use it for | Shows live state |
|---|---|---|
| `result` | Something finished: "Tunde beat Danny 😂" | No |
| `invite` | Asking someone to act. Buttons turn off once the interaction finishes | Yes |
| `status` | Ongoing progress: "4 of 12 rounds done" | Yes |

Older Zend versions that don't know a template show a plain **Open** card.

## Slots

| Slot | Rules |
|---|---|
| `title` | Required, up to 80 characters |
| `subtitle` | Up to 140 characters |
| `emoji` | One emoji |
| `media` | `https` image URL |
| `buttons` | Up to 2 |
| `check` | `result` cards only. Shows **Result checked ✓** when the interaction's [draws](/fairness/random-draws) verify |

You can't set amounts or participants. Zend fills those from the interaction. Text must follow the [copy guide](/ship/copy-guide).

## Buttons

Every button needs a `label` of 1–20 characters.

| `action` | Field | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| `open_sheet` | `path` | Opens your app at `path?interaction=<id>` |
| `start_interaction` | `type` | Opens your app at `/?start=<type>` (with `from=<id>`) |
| `open_url` | `url` | URL must be on your origins. Opens inside your app |

## Placement

- **`chat`**: a bubble in the DM between the poster and the other participant. Only for interactions with exactly two people (`CHAT_NEEDS_TWO`).
- **`feed`**: shows in **From your apps** at the top of Activity for everyone who can see the interaction. A `participants` interaction needs at least two people (`NOTHING_TO_SHOW`).

## Posting rules

- The posting user must be a participant and must have allowed posting on the consent sheet (`POSTING_NOT_ALLOWED`).
- Users who muted your app don't see or post your cards (`APP_MUTED`).
- Each user can post up to the lower of your tier's daily cap and your manifest's `limits.cardsPerUserPerDay` (`CARD_LIMIT`). See [Limits](/ship/limits).
- `idempotencyKey` is per user. Repeating it returns the existing card.
