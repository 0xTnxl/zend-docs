Zend opens your app full screen.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Mobile">
    - A WebView with no URL bar or browser chrome.
    - Zend shows your icon and name while you load.
    - The status bar matches your page's `body` background colour.
    - Zoom and link callouts are off.
    - One small floating pill (top right, collapses to a dot, draggable) gives the user **Close** and **Report**.
    - Android back goes back through your pages first.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Web">
    - The address bar shows `app.usezend.app/apps/<slug>`.
    - Your app fills the window under a slim Zend bar.
    - The browser's back button returns to Zend.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

Unreviewed apps show "Made by {you}. Not checked by Zend." on the loading screen and in the menu.

## Guidelines

- Build for full screen, mobile first. Set a solid `body` background colour.
- Keep the top-right 120×44 px clear of important controls. The Zend pill sits there until it folds.
- Don't add your own "back to Zend" button. Call `zend.close()` when you're done; the optional result goes back to whatever opened you.
- Links to other sites open outside Zend after the user confirms. Keep everything you need on your own origins.
- Users find you on Discover by name. Use your real app name in `name` and a one-line `description`.
- Let users report problems with `zend.report({ reason, detail?, interactionId?, prizeId? })`. Reasons: `scam`, `unfair_result`, `offensive`, `broken`, `other`.

## Hosting headers

Zend loads your page in a frame on the web. If you send a `Content-Security-Policy`, allow Zend as a frame ancestor:

```text
Content-Security-Policy: frame-ancestors https://app.usezend.app
```

Public apps also serve `/.well-known/zend-app.json` on every origin. See [Public apps](/ship/public-apps#prove-you-own-your-origins).
