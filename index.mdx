A **ZApp** is a web app you host on your own HTTPS origin. Zend opens it full screen inside the Zend mobile app and at `app.usezend.app`, and gives it Zend sign-in, money, shared state, realtime rooms and cards in chats and the Activity feed.

Zend draws every money screen and every card. Your app asks for a payment; Zend shows its own confirm sheet, the user decides, and your server hears about it through a signed webhook. Your code never handles balances, keys or payment UI.

## The pieces

| Piece | Where it lives | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `@zendfi/appkit` | Your page | Talks to Zend: identity, money, interactions, cards, live rooms |
| `@zendfi/appkit/server` | Your backend (Node 18+) | Verifies sessions and webhooks, moves interactions, resolves prizes |
| Manifest (`zapp.json`) | Uploaded to Zend | Declares your origins, permissions, chat actions and state machines |
| `zend` CLI | Your terminal and CI | Creates apps, checks and deploys manifests, manages keys |

All HTTP paths in these docs are under `https://api.usezend.app/api/zend/v2`.
