Your app never touches balances, keys or payment screens. It asks; Zend shows its own confirm sheet with your app's name, the amount, the recipient, and where the money goes; the user decides.

## Actions

|  | [`pay`](/money/pay) | [`request`](/money/requests-and-links#ask-links) | [`createLink`](/money/requests-and-links#money-links) | [`prize.create`](/money/prizes) | [`unlock.buy`](/money/unlock) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Direction | User → a Zend user | Anyone → the user | User → whoever opens the link | Host → winners your server picks | Buyer → the item's payees |
| Permission | `pay` | `request_payment` | `create_link` | `prize` | `unlock` |
| You get back | `paymentId` | `id`, `url` | `id` (Zend shares the link) | The prize | `paymentId`, `receiptId` |
| Webhook | `payment.completed` | `payment.completed` per payer | `link.claimed` per collector | `prize.*` | `unlock.bought`, `payment.completed` |
| Minimum | $0.01 | $0.01 | $0.10 per person | $1 | $0.01 per payee |

## Amounts

Every money call takes either `amount` in US dollars or `price: { ngn }` in Naira:

```ts
await zend.pay({ to: "@ada", amount: 1.5 });
await zend.pay({ to: "@ada", price: { ngn: 2000 } });
```

Money is held and moved in dollars. Zend's sheets show each amount in the person's own currency (chosen from where they signed up, changeable in **Settings → Currency**). A price you set in Naira shows in Naira. With `price`, Zend converts at its current rate. Webhooks carry `amount_usd`, plus `price_ngn` and the rate used when you priced in Naira.

Naira is the only currency you can price in. Other keys in `price` are ignored, and the call then fails with `INVALID_AMOUNT`. Your app can't read a person's display currency.

An ask link priced in Naira asks for that many Naira each time someone pays it. Money links and prizes are converted once, when they're made.

## reference

Pass `reference` (up to 64 characters), such as your order or game ID. It comes back on every webhook for that money action, and you can look payments up by it from your server.

## Rules Zend enforces

- Your app's [tier limits](/ship/limits): payment size, daily spend per user, links per day, link size.
- Zend-wide limits on links and prizes: today a money link holds at most $20 and a prize is at most $20, whatever your tier.
- Only one money sheet at a time. A second call while one is open rejects with `BUSY`.
- `USER_CANCELLED` means the user tapped Cancel. It's not an error to report.

<Warning>
Never treat a resolved `pay()` in the browser as proof of payment. Credit orders on the `payment.completed` webhook. See [Verifying payments](/money/verifying-payments).
</Warning>

## Entry fees and stakes

Player-paid entry fees and stakes are off by platform policy. Host-funded [prizes](/money/prizes) work today.
