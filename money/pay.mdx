---
title: "Take a payment"
description: "Send money from the current user to another Zend user with zend.pay."
---

`zend.pay` asks the current user to send money to a Zend user. Zend checks your limits, shows its confirm sheet, sends the money, and records the payment against your app.

```ts
const { paymentId, status } = await zend.pay({
  to: "@ada",
  price: { ngn: 2000 },
  note: "Article: How to win at Ludo",
  reference: "article_7",
});
```

Needs the `pay` permission.

## Parameters

<ParamField body="to" type="`@${string}`" required>
  The recipient's username, with `@`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="amount" type="number">
  US dollars, from 0.01. Give `amount` or `price`, not both.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="price" type="{ ngn: number }">
  Price in Naira. Zend converts at its current rate and shows ₦ on its sheet.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="note" type="string">
  Shown to both people.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="reference" type="string">
  Up to 64 characters. Comes back on the webhook.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="interactionId" type="string">
  The interaction this payment is part of. With a [spend allowance](/money/allowance), payments to other people in it can skip the confirm sheet.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="splits" type="object[]">
  Pay others from the same payment. See [Split payments](/money/splits).
</ParamField>

## Returns

`{ paymentId, status }`. Use it to update your UI, not to grant anything.

## Confirm on your server

`payment.completed` reaches your webhook even if the user closes your app right after paying.

```json
{
  "type": "payment.completed",
  "data": {
    "payment_id": "…",
    "from": "zu_...",
    "to": "zu_...",
    "amount_usd": 1.32,
    "price_ngn": 2000,
    "ngn_per_usd": 1515.15,
    "rate_id": "…",
    "reference": "article_7"
  }
}
```

Before recording it, Zend checks the payer, recipient and amount against what your app asked for. See [Verifying payments](/money/verifying-payments).

## Errors

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `USER_CANCELLED` | The user tapped Cancel |
| `BUSY` | Another money sheet is open |
| `PERMISSION_DENIED` | Your manifest lacks `pay` |
| `INVALID_AMOUNT` | Amount missing or out of range |
| `APP_LIMIT_AMOUNT` | Over your tier's payment size |
| `APP_LIMIT_DAILY` | Over your tier's daily spend for this user |
| `NOT_CONNECTED` | The user hasn't continued to your app |
