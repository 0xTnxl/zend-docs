---
title: "Split payments"
description: "Pay several people, including your app, from one payment."
---

A split sends parts of one payment to other people in the same go. Use it to take a platform fee, or to pay a writer and an editor together.

```ts
// Ada gets ₦1,800 and your app gets ₦200.
await zend.pay({
  to: "@ada",
  price: { ngn: 2000 },
  splits: [{ to: "app", percent: 10 }],
  reference: "article_7",
});
```

## Rules

- Up to 3 splits.
- Each split goes to `"app"` or an `@username`, and has either `percent` (above 0, below 100) or `amount` in US dollars.
- `to` gets whatever is left. Every part, including the rest, must be at least $0.01.
- Each person can appear once.
- Zend shows every part on its confirm sheet before the user confirms.

Invalid splits fail with `SPLIT_INVALID`.

## Your app's payout account

`"app"` sends money to the Zend account you choose as your app's payout account. Set it once with the CLI:

```bash
zend apps payout <slug> <username>
```

Until it's set, splits to `"app"` fail with `APP_PAYOUT_NOT_SET`.

## Webhook

The `payment.completed` event for a split payment includes every part in `parts`, alongside the usual `amount_usd` total and `reference`.
