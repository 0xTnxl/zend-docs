---
title: "Copy guide"
description: "Plain-language rules for anything your users read."
---

Zend users shouldn't need to know how money moves. Anything they read in your app, your labels and your cards should sound like messaging, not banking or crypto.

## Avoid

Sui, blockchain, chain, on-chain, crypto, token, coin, USDC, stablecoin, wallet, wallet address, address, gas, network fee, transaction, digest, signature, signing, key, seed, escrow, contract, smart contract, zkLogin, proof, explorer.

Zend rejects the main ones in your manifest's `name`, chat action labels, `description`, `developer.name`, and in card text.

## Say instead

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| wallet, balance | your balance, your Zend |
| transfer | send, pay, Zend |
| escrow link, give link | link, money link |
| claim | collect |
| reclaim | take back |
| expired and returned | expired, money returned |
| escrow, contract-held | held safely until the rules say who gets it |
| sent to the creator's wallet | goes straight to {name}. Zend can't get it back. |
| share wallet address | can send money straight to you |
| sign the transaction | confirm it's you |
| transaction failed | That didn't go through. Try again. |

## Other rules

- Show amounts in Naira where you can.
- Keep labels short: chat actions up to 24 characters, buttons up to 20.
- Error `message` fields from Zend are for your logs. Show your own copy to users.
