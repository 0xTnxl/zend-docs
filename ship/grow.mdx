On the Zend network, your app starts where people already talk and send money. You don't need ads or an invite system; you need to use the surfaces Zend gives every app.

## Be one tap away in every chat

A [chat action](/guides/manifest#chatactions) puts your app in the **Apps** tray of every DM. Tapping it starts an interaction with the other person and opens your app. Name it for what people do ("Challenge", "Split", "Flip"), not for your brand.

## Make every result an invite

Post a [card](/guides/cards) when something happens. The other person sees it in the chat and can tap straight back in.

- A `result` card with a `start_interaction` button ("Play again", "Rematch") starts the next round with the same people.
- An `invite` card asks someone to act and switches off its buttons when the interaction ends.
- A `status` card shows progress ("4 of 12 rounds done") and updates itself.
- With `placement: "feed"`, cards also appear in **From your apps** for everyone who can see the interaction. Use `friends` visibility to reach people's friends.

## Bring in people who aren't using your app yet

- The [people picker](/guides/people-picker) returns `pick` tokens that work as participants even for people who've never opened your app.
- `https://app.usezend.app/apps/<slug>` and `https://app.usezend.app/i/<id>` open your app for anyone, after they sign in to Zend if they need to.
- A [money link](/money/requests-and-links#money-links) works for people who aren't on Zend at all. They sign up to collect it.

## Get listed

[Public apps](/ship/public-apps) are listed on Discover, by `category`, with your `name` and one-line `description`. They also lose the "Not checked by Zend" label and move to higher [limits](/ship/limits).

## Keep people playing

- Ask once for a [spend allowance](/money/allowance) so small payments in a game don't need the confirm sheet every time.
- Use [live rooms](/guides/live-rooms) so both people see moves as they happen.
- Keep cards honest and specific. People mute apps that post too much, and muted apps can't post to them.

## Measure on your server

Every interaction sends `interaction.created` and `interaction.updated` webhooks, and every payment sends `payment.completed`. Count them by `type` and `reference` to see what people start, finish and pay for.
