## Open it on your phone

Every ZApp has a link: `https://app.usezend.app/apps/<slug>`. Open it on a phone with Zend installed and it opens inside the app.

The fastest way to get it onto your phone is a QR code in your terminal:

```bash
npx qrcode-terminal https://app.usezend.app/apps/hello
```

Scan it with your phone's camera. The first time, Zend asks you to continue to your app.

To open it on a specific interaction, use `https://app.usezend.app/i/<interaction id>`.

## Test from a chat

Chat actions only appear in a chat. Open a DM with a friend (or a second account), tap **+ → Apps**, and pick your action. To let a friend open your app while it's private:

```bash
zend apps testers add hello danny
```

## Develop against your local machine

Zend only opens HTTPS origins listed in your manifest. To point it at your laptop:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a tunnel">
    Use any HTTPS tunnel, such as `cloudflared tunnel --url http://localhost:5173` or `ngrok http 5173`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the tunnel to your manifest">
    Add the tunnel's origin to `origins` (up to 5) and redeploy:

    ```json
    "origins": ["https://hello.example.com", "https://quiet-river-1234.trycloudflare.com"]
    ```

    ```bash
    zend apps deploy zapp.json
    ```
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the tunnel's page in Zend">
    Chat actions open `sheet` paths on your **first** origin, so put the tunnel first while you develop, then move your real origin back before you ship.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Debug

- **On a laptop:** open `https://app.usezend.app/apps/<slug>` in a desktop browser. Your app runs in a frame, so your browser's dev tools show its console and network requests.
- **On a phone:** add an in-page console such as [Eruda](https://github.com/liriliri/eruda) while you develop, and remove it before you ship.
- **Webhooks:** call `GET /apps/server/events` with your app key to see what Zend sent and when it was delivered.
